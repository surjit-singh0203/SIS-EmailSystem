Vessel/Tank Pump Setting

Vessel/Tank Pump Setting

These settings refer to adding of various tanks and pumps onboard a particular vessel. To access this page, click on “SETTING” on the Navigation page and click on “Vessel/Tank Pump Setting”. Various Vessels will be displayed on this page.

 

 

 

To add vessel’s Pumps detail:

  • Click on “View” button below Pump column against a desired Vessel.
  • Here, we can Add/View/Edit/Delete/Sort all pumps on a vessel.
  • After adding click on “Save Order” to save the data.
  • Click continuously on the arrow icon on the left side of each row, drag row upwards or downwards to rank the sorting order on this list. This sorting order will determine the list serial order display of these items in all the reports.

Note: Also, after reshuffling the order of Pumps, Kindly Click on “Save Order” for final Submission.

 

 

 

 

To add a new pump, click on “Add Pump” and fill in the desired details. Click on “Submit” to save the data.

 

a)       Pump Category*: It refers to the classification of a pump in relation to their role onboard a vessel.

b)      Pump Type*: It refers to the working principle of a pump.

c)       Name*: It refers to the name of a pump designated onboard a vessel.

d)      Capacity(m3/hr) *: It refers to the pump discharge capacity.

To add vessel’s Tanks detail:

Click on “View” button below Tank column against a desired Vessel.

Here, we can Add/View all Tanks on a vessel.

Maintaining RoBs: It refers to different design and structures of vessels i.e., If a vessel does not have mentioned tanks/spaces or ROB’s are not to be considered then under “Maintaining ROBs” the same should be selected as “NO”. This will allow the user to not add or view that particular tank.

User can add/view all tanks by clicking against a desired tank type.

 

To add a new tank, click on “Add” button against a desired tank type and fill in the required details. Upon adding all tanks and their details click on “Save All” to submit the data.

 

 

 

a)       Cargo Tank Name*: It refers to the name of cargo tank designated on board a vessel.

b)      Cargo Tanks Height in meters*: It refers to the height in metres of a cargo tank.

c)       Cargo Tanks Capacity in m3*: It refers to the cargo carrying capacity of a cargo tank.

 

 

To view details of any tank type, click on “View” button against a desired tank type.

Here user can add/edit/delete the tanks against an underlined tank type, after adding click on “Save Order” to save the data.

Click continuously on the arrow icon on the left side of each row, drag row upwards or downwards to rank the sorting order on this list. This sorting order will determine the list serial order display of these items in all the reports.

Note: Also, after reshuffling the order of tanks, Kindly Click on “Save Order” for final Submission.
