Prepare Loading Report

When you click on “Add Loading Report” button, it opens a form where the following fields are to be filled for adding a new report ----

a)       Voyage Number: A Voyage Number is a unique number used to identify a voyage. This is used for informational purposes. The Vessel ID identifies an actual equipment or ship that is used in the voyage schedule.

b)      Leg: A voyage leg is defined as a voyage between ports.

c)       Port: It refers to a port where vessel discharges its cargo.

d)      Report Date& Time: It refers to a date and time on which the Report is prepared.

e)      Draft Fwd. (Mtrs): The draft forward (bow) is a draft measured in Meters at the perpendicular of the bow.

f)        Draft Mid (Mtrs): The draft mid is a draft measured in Meters at the midship.

g)       Draft Aft (Mtrs): The draft aft (stern) is draft measured in Meters at the perpendicular of the stern.

h)      ETD Date & Time: Estimated time of departure refers to a date and time when the vessel is expected/estimated to depart from the port. 

·         Cargo-1

a)       Cargo Grades: It refers to the grade of cargo onboard.

b)      Commence Loading Date & Time: It refers to starting date and time for commencement of Cargo Loading operations.

c)       Terminal Loading Rate(m3/hr): It refers to a rate at which a terminal/port can load cargo on a vessel.

d)      Loading Rate Accepted(m3/hr): It refers to the rate accepted by the vessel at which the cargo is to be loaded via terminal.

e)      Average Achieved Loading Rate(m3/hr): It refers to an average rate of cargo loading by terminal throughout its loading operation.

f)        No of Manifold / Hoses by Terminal: It refers to the Number of Manifolds/hoses used by the terminal to load cargo.

g)       Size of Manifold / Hoses by Terminal (Inches): It refers to the size in inches of manifold/hose used by the terminal to load cargo.

h)      No of Manifold / Hoses by Vessel: It refers to the Number of Manifolds/hoses used by the vessel to load cargo.

i)        Size of Manifold / Hoses by Vessel (Inches): It refers to the size in inches of manifold/hose used by the vessel to load cargo.

j)        Shore Line Distance (mtrs): It refers to the distance from the ships manifold to the shore tank.

k)       Quantity onboard (MT): It refers to the amount of Cargo loaded by terminal on board a vessel.

l)        Balance Quantity to be Loaded (MT): It refers to the amount of cargo remaining to be loaded by the terminal.

m)    ETC / Actual Comp Date & Time: It refers to the estimated/actual completion date and time for loading operations.

·         Cargo-2

a)       Letter of Protests: It refers to a written communication recording dissatisfaction on the part of one party concerning any operational matter over which the recipient or other party has control and holding the other party responsible for any consequences of the matter being complained about.

§  Time: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding time management.

§  Rate: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding cargo discharge rate.

§  Hose Connection: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding hose connection during operations.

§  High H2S: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding High Hydrogen Sulphide gas during operations.

b)      Ballast Pumps in Use: It refers to the ballast pumps which are used during Cargo discharge operations

i)        Rate: It refers to the flow rate at which the ballast pumps are used during operation.

c)       Stoppage Reason: It refers to a reason for any halt/interruption during discharge operations.

i)        Date Time From: It refers to a start date & time for any stoppage during operations.

ii)       Date Time To: It refers to an end date & time for any stoppage during operations.

·         Loading Report Remarks.: It refers to any additional activity/notice/comment/observation/declaration etc. to be added in the report.
