Prepare Discharging Report

When you click on “Add Discharging Report” button, it opens a form where the following fields are to be filled for adding a new report ----

 

a)       Voyage Number: A Voyage Number is a unique number used to identify a voyage. This is used for informational purposes. The Vessel ID identifies an actual equipment or ship that is used in the voyage schedule.

b)      Leg: A voyage leg is defined as a voyage between ports.

c)       Port: It refers to a port where vessel discharges its cargo.

d)      Report Date& Time: It refers to a date and time on which the Report is prepared.

e)      Draft Fwd. (Mtrs): The draft forward (bow) is a draft measured in Meters at the perpendicular of the bow.

f)        Draft Mid (Mtrs): The draft mid is a draft measured in Meters at the midship.

g)       Draft Aft (Mtrs): The draft aft (stern) is draft measured in Meters at the perpendicular of the stern.

h)      ETD Date & Time: Estimated time of departure refers to a date and time when the vessel is expected/estimated to depart from the port. 

 

 

 

·         Cargo-1

a)       Cargo Grades: It refers to the grade of cargo onboard.

b)      Commence Discharge Date & Time: It refers to starting date and time for commencement of Cargo discharge operations.

c)       Terminal Acceptable Discharging Rate: It refers to a desired rate at which a terminal/port can receive cargo.

d)      Discharging Pressure Requested: It refers to the pressure requested by the vessel to the terminal at which the cargo is to be discharged.

e)      Average Discharge Rate By Vessel: It refers to an average rate of cargo discharge by vessel throughout its discharging operation on terminal.

f)        Average Discharge pressure By Vessel: It refers to an average cargo discharge pressure by vessel throughout its discharging operation on terminal.

g)       No of Pumps in use: It refers to the number of pumps used for discharging cargo.

h)      No of Manifold / Hoses by Terminal: It refers to the Number of Manifolds/hoses used by the terminal to receive cargo.

i)        Size of Manifold / Hoses by Terminal (Inches): It refers to the size in inches of manifold/hose used by the terminal to receive cargo.

j)        No of Manifold / Hoses by Vessel: It refers to the Number of Manifolds/hoses used by the vessel to discharge cargo.

k)       Size of Manifold / Hoses by Vessel (Inches): It refers to the size in inches of manifold/hose used by the vessel to discharge cargo.

l)        Total Cargo Discharged: It refers to the amount of Cargo discharged at the end of the discharging operation.

m)    Balance Cargo to be Discharged: It refers to the amount of cargo remaining on board to be discharged in further voyages.

n)      ETC / Actual Comp Date & Time: It refers to the estimated/actual completion date and time of discharging operation.

 

·         Cargo-2

a)       Ballast Pumps in Use: It refers to the ballast pumps which are used during Cargo discharge operations

i)        Rate: It refers to the flow rate at which the ballast pumps are used during operation.

b)      Cargo Pumps in Use: It refers to the cargo pumps which are used during Cargo discharge operations

i)        Rate: It refers to the flow rate at which the cargo pumps are used during operation.

c)       Power Packs: It refers to a self-contained unit that consists mainly of a motor, a reservoir and a hydraulic pump using fluid to transmit power from one location to another. Hydraulic power packs can generate massive amounts of power which can be used to drive hydraulic machinery.

i)        No of Power Packs onboard: It refers to the number of power packs onboard a vessel.

ii)       No of Power Packs used: It refers to the number of power packs used for discharging operations.

d)      Letter of Protests: It refers to a written communication recording dissatisfaction on the part of one party concerning any operational matter over which the recipient or other party has control and holding the other party responsible for any consequences of the matter being complained about.

i)        Time: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding time management.

ii)       Rate: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding cargo discharge rate.

iii)     Hose Connection: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding hose connection during operations.

iv)     High H2S: It refers to an issuance of a “Letter of Protest” regarding High Hydrogen Sulphide gas during operations.

e)      Stoppage Reason: It refers to a reason for any halt/interruption during discharge operations.

i)        Date Time From: It refers to a start date & time for any stoppage during operations.

ii)       Date Time To: It refers to an end date & time for any stoppage during operations.

 

f)        Discharge Report Remarks.: It refers to any additional activity/notice/comment/observation/declaration etc. to be added in the report.
