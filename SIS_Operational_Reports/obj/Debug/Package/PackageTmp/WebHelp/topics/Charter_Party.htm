Charter Party

A charterparty is a maritime contract between a shipowner and a "charterer" for the hire of either a ship for the carriage of passengers or cargo.

It means that the charter party will clearly and unambiguously set out the rights and responsibilities of the ship owner and the charterers and any subsequent dispute between them will be settled in the court of law or any agreed forum with reference to the agreed terms and conditions as embodied in the charter party.

To access the details of a charter party, click on “SETTING” and then “Charter Party”. Here you can add new CP Contract, Edit/Delete past CP Contracts and View Detail of the concerned CP Contract.

To Add new CP Contract, click on “Add CP Contract” and fill in the required details.

 

 

 

 

After filling in the desired details, click on “Submit” to save the details.

 

 

a)       Charter Party ID*: It is a unique number given by the office to a Charter Party Contract.

b)      Charter Party Name*: It is a unique name given by the office to a Charter Party Contract.

c)       Start Date*: It refers to a date for starting a Charter Party Contract.

d)      End Date*: It refers to a date for ending a Charter Party Contract.

e)      Voyage Type*: It refers to the type of charter a vessel is entitled.

Below section is to be filled on every new Charter Party Contract.

Click on “View detail” against desired Charter party ID. A page listing all the information regarding the current charter party will appear on the screen.

 

 

Office can add/edit/delete Vessels, Daily Fuel Consumption (MTS/DAY) & Other Consumption (MTS/DAY) for the underlined Charter Party ID.

 

 

  To add vessels, click on “Add Vessels” and fill in the desired details. Select the Start date and End date for the vessel in a Charter Party contract.

 

To add Main Consumption of a vessel per day, click on “Add Main Consumption” and fill in the desired details.

       Office needs to define Speed criteria’s for a vessel thereby calculating its Maine Engine (ME) & Auxiliary Engine (AE) Consumption per day. To add different criteria’s click on “Add” to do the same. Click “Submit” to save all entries.

 

 

To add other Consumption of a vessel per day, click on “Add Other Consumption” and fill in the desired details.

     Office needs to define different consumption criteria’s for a vessel thereby calculating its Auxiliary Engine (AE) per day Consumption of VLSFO/MDO and Boiler per day Consumption of MDO. To add different criteria’s click on “Add” to do the same. Click       “Submit” to save all entries.
